# Visiting Brussels

There are several excellent resources for visitors and tourists to Brussels. The [visit.brussels website](https://www.visit.brussels/en/visitors/) contains information on everything you need to know, including:

- ✈️ [Traveling to and from the city](https://www.visit.brussels/en/visitors/plan-your-trip/practical-info/how-to-get-to-brussels)
- 🏨 [Where to stay](https://www.visit.brussels/en/visitors/where-to-stay/all-accommodations)
- 🍽️ [Restaurants, bars, and cafes](https://www.visit.brussels/en/visitors/where-to-eat/places-to-eat)
- 💃 [Things to do](https://www.visit.brussels/en/visitors/what-to-do)

The [city of Brussels](https://www.brussels.be/tourism) also provides helpful information for tourists including:

 - 🗺️ [City maps](https://www.brussels.be/geoportal)
 - 👮 [Police and emergency services info](https://www.brussels.be/police)
 - 🌍 [Embassies and Consulates](https://diplomatie.belgium.be/en/embassies-and-consulates)
 - 💻 [How to connect to free city-wide Wifi](https://be.brussels/fr/propos-de-la-region/valeurs-budget-et-strategie/transparence-libre-acces-linformation/wifi-gratuit-bruxelles#Ou-consulter-gratuitement-Internet-5864)

You can also view a [listing of events](https://www.visit.brussels/en/visitors/agenda/all-events-wizard?eventDate=20260127-20260203) happening in Brussels during the week and weekend of FOSDEM.
